01 — ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understand what happens
inside your network.

The Network Forensics Analysis System is a web-based project that helps examine network packets and organize security-related observations in one interface. It combines packet inspection, rule-based suspicious activity checks, visual summaries and report generation to support investigation.

Users can monitor supported live traffic, upload authorized packet capture files, review packet information and examine recorded threat indicators. Results depend on the available traffic, capture contents and detection rules.

ⓘ

Important: A suspicious indicator is a reason to investigate, not proof that an attack succeeded. This project analyzes and reports activity; it does not automatically block attackers.

04 — USER GUIDE

Get started in four steps.

Follow these steps to use the system responsibly and understand its output.

01

Sign in to the application

Open the application and sign in with your registered account to access protected pages.

02

Choose your analysis method

Open Live Capture to inspect supported live traffic, or open Reports to upload a saved PCAP/PCAPNG file.

03

Review packets and alerts

Examine protocols, IP addresses, packet sizes and flagged activity. Check the surrounding context before drawing conclusions.

04

Save or export findings

Use the available report, filter and export options to document your investigation.

⌘
RESPONSIBLE USE

Investigate with care.

  • Analyze only traffic you own or have permission to inspect.
  • Use valid, trusted PCAP/PCAPNG files for upload testing.
  • Do not share captures containing private or sensitive data.
  • Treat alerts as indicators that require verification.
  • Remember that this application reports activity; it does not automatically block traffic.
